# Billing and Cancellation

## Billing

- Plans are billed **monthly in advance**, on the same day of the month the
  service started.
- Payment methods: credit or debit card, or bank transfer (ACH). The payment method
  can be updated in the app: Account > Billing > Payment method.
- Invoices are available in the app for the last 24 months.

## Late payments

- Payments are due on the billing date. There is a **10-day grace period** with no
  late fee.
- After the grace period, a **$10 late fee** is added once per missed payment.
- If a bill is unpaid **30 days** after the billing date, Contoso sends a final
  notice by email and SMS. Service, including alarm monitoring, is suspended
  **7 days after the final notice** if the bill is still unpaid.
- Service is restored within 1 hour after the overdue balance is paid.

## Cancellation

- Only the **account owner** can cancel service, by phone or chat with a support
  agent. Cancellation can't be done through the support assistant.
- **Month-to-month:** cancel any time with **30 days' notice**. Service continues
  until the end of the notice period.
- **12-month agreement:** cancelling before the end of the agreement incurs an
  **early termination fee** equal to the waived $149 installation fee, prorated for
  the months remaining (for example, 6 months remaining = $74.50).
- Customers who move within the Contoso service area can transfer their service to
  the new home instead of cancelling, with no early termination fee.

## Refunds

- Monthly fees are not refunded for partial months.
- Exceptions: service credits for Contoso outages (see
  `outages-and-service-status.md`) and billing errors.
- Billing errors are refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 business
  days after confirmation.
