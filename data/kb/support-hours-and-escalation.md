# Support Hours, Callbacks and Escalation

## Emergencies first

If there is smoke, fire, a carbon monoxide alarm, a break-in, or anyone is in
danger, the customer should **leave the home if it's unsafe and call 911 first**.
The Monitoring Center (for monitored accounts) is available 24/7 regardless of plan.

## Support hours (phone and chat)

| Plan | Hours |
|---|---|
| Essentials | Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–6 p.m. Central |
| Plus | Daily, 7 a.m.–10 p.m. Central |
| Premium | 24/7 |

Support is closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day for Essentials and Plus.
Alarm monitoring is never closed.

## Callback requests

Customers can request a callback in the Contoso Home app (Help > Request callback)
or through the support assistant.
- **Essentials and Plus:** callback within 1 business day.
- **Premium:** callback within 2 hours, any time.
A callback request needs the customer's name, phone number, and a short
description of the issue.

## When a human support agent takes over

The support assistant hands the conversation to a human agent, with a summary of
the conversation, when:
- The customer reports an emergency (after telling them to call 911)
- The customer asks to speak with a person
- The customer wants to cancel service or change the account owner
- The customer disputes a charge over $100
- An on-site technician visit may be needed
- A hub shows a solid red light (hardware fault)
- The assistant can't find the answer in Contoso's documentation

## What the support assistant cannot do

For security, the support assistant cannot change the verbal passcode, turn off
alarm monitoring, cancel service, issue refunds, or unlock doors remotely.
These require the account owner and, in most cases, a human agent.
