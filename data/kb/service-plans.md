# Contoso Home Services — Service Plans

Contoso Home Services installs and manages smart home systems built around the
**Contoso Home Hub**: sensors, cameras, smart locks, thermostats and lights,
controlled from the Contoso Home app. Every plan includes hub management,
automatic firmware updates and the Contoso Home app.

## Plans

| | Essentials | Plus | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly price | $24 | $39 | $59 |
| Connected devices | Up to 25 | Up to 60 | Unlimited |
| 24/7 professional alarm monitoring | Add-on ($15/month) | Included | Included |
| Cellular backup for the hub | Not available | Included | Included |
| Cloud video recording | 7 days, up to 2 cameras | 30 days, up to 4 cameras | 60 days, up to 10 cameras |
| Support hours | Mon–Fri, 8 a.m.–6 p.m. Central | Daily, 7 a.m.–10 p.m. Central | 24/7 |
| Callback after a request | Within 1 business day | Within 1 business day | Within 2 hours |
| Free on-site technician visit | No | No | 1 per year |

Prices are per month, per home, and exclude taxes.

## Add-ons

- **Professional monitoring for Essentials:** $15/month. Includes 24/7 alarm
  monitoring but not cellular backup.
- **Extra camera storage:** $3 per camera per month above the plan's camera limit,
  at the plan's retention period.
- **On-site technician visit (outside the Premium free visit):** $89 per visit.

## Installation and agreements

- **Month-to-month:** no long-term commitment. One-time installation fee: $149.
- **12-month agreement:** installation fee waived. Early termination fees apply
  (see `billing-and-cancellation.md`).
- Equipment is purchased by the customer and remains theirs after cancellation.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the current month.
Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. If a downgrade would
put the home over the new plan's device or camera limit, the customer must remove
devices or buy extra camera storage first.

## Service area

Contoso Home Services currently serves the United States only.
