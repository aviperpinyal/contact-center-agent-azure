# Cameras, Video Recording and Privacy

## How recording works

Contoso cameras record a clip to the Contoso cloud whenever they detect motion.
Clips are viewable in the Contoso Home app under **Cameras > History**.
Continuous (24/7) recording is not offered.

## How long video is kept

| Plan | Retention | Cameras included |
|---|---|---|
| Essentials | 7 days | Up to 2 |
| Plus | 30 days | Up to 4 |
| Premium | 60 days | Up to 10 |

Extra cameras can be added for $3 per camera per month at the plan's retention
period. Clips older than the retention period are deleted automatically.

## Who can see the video

- **The customer and the people they invite** in the app (Settings > Users).
  The account owner controls who has access.
- **Contoso support staff cannot view video** unless the account owner grants
  temporary support access in the app (Settings > Privacy > Support access).
  Support access expires automatically after **24 hours** and can be revoked at any
  time.
- **Monitoring Center operators** can view clips from the moments around an
  active alarm, only if the customer has turned on **video verification**
  (Settings > Monitoring > Video verification). This helps operators confirm a
  real emergency before dispatching.

## Where video is stored

Video is encrypted in transit and at rest and stored in data centers in the
United States.

## Deleting video

- Customers can delete individual clips or all clips at any time in the app.
- When an account is closed, all video is permanently deleted within **30 days**.
- Deleted clips cannot be recovered.

## Requests from law enforcement

Contoso Home Services shares customer video with law enforcement only with the
account owner's consent or when required by a valid legal order.
