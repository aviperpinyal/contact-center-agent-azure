# Contoso Home Services — Professional Alarm Monitoring

Professional monitoring is included in the **Plus** and **Premium** plans and is
available as a $15/month add-on for **Essentials**. The Contoso Monitoring Center
is staffed 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

## What happens when an alarm goes off

1. The hub sends the alarm signal to the Monitoring Center.
2. An operator calls the **primary contact** on the account, usually within
   60 seconds.
3. The person who answers must give the account's **verbal passcode**.
4. If the correct passcode is given and the person confirms a false alarm, the
   operator cancels the alarm. No emergency services are sent.
5. If nobody answers, the operator calls the **secondary contact**.
6. If no contact answers, the wrong passcode is given, or a contact reports an
   emergency, the operator **dispatches emergency services** (police, fire or
   medical) to the home address.

## Always-on monitoring

Smoke, fire, carbon monoxide (CO) and water-leak sensors are monitored 24/7,
**even when the security system is disarmed**. Intrusion sensors (doors, windows,
motion) are monitored only while the system is armed.

## Verbal passcode

- Every monitored account has one verbal passcode, set during installation.
- For security, the passcode can only be changed by the **account owner**, either
  in the Contoso Home app (Settings > Monitoring > Passcode) or by phone with a
  support agent after identity verification.
- Contoso staff never ask for the passcode by email or text message.

## Test mode

Before testing sensors or alarms, put the system in **test mode**: Contoso Home app
> Monitoring > Test mode. Test mode lasts up to 4 hours. During test mode, alarm
signals reach the Monitoring Center but no emergency services are dispatched.
Monitoring returns to normal automatically when test mode ends.

## False alarms

Contoso Home Services does not charge for false alarms. Some cities and counties
fine homeowners for repeated false alarm dispatches; those fines are the
customer's responsibility. To reduce false alarms, use test mode before testing,
and keep the contact list and passcode up to date.

## Internet outages

If the home internet goes down, the **cellular backup** in Plus and Premium keeps
alarm signals flowing to the Monitoring Center. Essentials does not include
cellular backup, so monitoring stops while the home has no internet connection.
